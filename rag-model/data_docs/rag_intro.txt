RAG stands for Retrieval Augmented Generation. 
It is a technique that improves the factual accuracy of language models by connecting them with external knowledge sources.

A RAG pipeline consists of two main components: 
a retriever and a generator. 
The retriever searches for relevant documents or chunks from a database using vector embeddings. 
The generator then uses these retrieved texts as context to produce a more accurate answer.

RAG is widely used in enterprise search, chatbots, and knowledge assistants.
