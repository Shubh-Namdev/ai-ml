A Large Language Model (LLM) is a deep learning model trained on large text corpora to understand and generate human-like language. 
Examples include GPT, Llama, and Mistral.

LLMs are not databases; they do not store facts in structured form. 
Instead, they learn statistical relationships between words and concepts.

When combined with retrieval methods, LLMs can access up-to-date or domain-specific knowledge.
